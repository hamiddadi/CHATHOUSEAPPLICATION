## Full description (≤4000 chars — both stores)

```
Chathouse is where conversations happen live. Join audio rooms on the topics you
care about, raise your hand to speak, and meet people around you on the map.

• LIVE AUDIO ROOMS — open, social, or closed rooms. Listen in, request to speak,
  react with emojis, and chat alongside the conversation.
• PEOPLE NEARBY — see who's online around you on the map and start talking.
• FOLLOW & MESSAGE — follow people you like and send direct messages once you
  both follow each other.
• CLUBS & EVENTS — join clubs, schedule rooms, and get reminded before they start.
• VOICE MESSAGES — send quick async voice notes when a live room isn't the moment.

Your safety comes first: report content or users, block anyone, and mute or
remove disruptive speakers. Reports enter the moderation queue for review.
You're always in control of your data — export it or delete your account anytime.

Download Chathouse and join the conversation.
```

## Content / age rating

> Answer each questionnaire **truthfully** — the rating is generated from the
> answers. Below is what to expect for a live, user-generated audio social app.

### Apple (App Store Connect → Age Rating)

- Complete Apple's **current** questionnaire in App Store Connect; do not reuse
  a legacy predicted `17+` result.
- Declare user communication, messaging, public UGC and live audio.
- For every mature-content category, answer from the realistic possible
  frequency in live user content. Do not answer `None` merely because the
  publisher does not create that content.
- Record the rating generated by App Store Connect in the release evidence.

### Mobile monetization review note

Digital purchases initiated in the current iOS/Android builds: **No**. Premium
checkout, Stripe billing-portal links and tips are hidden on both platforms.
Existing server-side entitlements and read-only purchase history may still be
displayed, but paid-only functionality is not unlocked in these builds.

### Google (Play Console → Content rating → IARC questionnaire)

- Category: **Social / Communication**
- Users interact / communicate: **Yes**
- User-generated content shared: **Yes**
- Shares user's location: **Yes**
- Digital purchases initiated in this build: **No**
- Declare the realistic possible frequency of objectionable language, sexual,
  violent and other mature user content in chat and live audio.
- Do not pre-select an expected `Teen` result; retain the IARC certificate
  generated from the final answers as release evidence.

### Required UGC declarations (both stores)

Verify every item end to end in the submitted binaries before declaring it:
report individual content and users, block users, mute/kick speakers, operate a
moderator queue (suspend / force-end), publish a reviewed zero-tolerance EULA,
and capture acceptance of the current Terms and Privacy notices. A backend
endpoint or draft document alone is not proof that the store build exposes the
control or that a moderation team is operational.
