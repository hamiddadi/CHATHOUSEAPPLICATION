ChatHouse, operated by [full registered legal name], prohibits child sexual
exploitation and abuse ("CSAE") and child sexual abuse material ("CSAM") on all
parts of its service. This prohibition applies even though ChatHouse is not
directed to children and account holders must be at least 16 (or the higher
minimum age required locally).

## Prohibited conduct

Users must not:

- create, upload, possess, request, distribute, advertise or facilitate CSAM;
- groom, coerce, extort, traffic or sexually exploit a child;
- sexualize a minor or solicit sexual images, audio, video or meetings from a
  minor;
- threaten to share sexual content involving a minor;
- link to, coordinate, normalize or help another person evade detection for
  CSAE or CSAM.

## Reporting

Users can report a user or room from within the ChatHouse application. Select
the child-safety or closest available reason and provide only information
necessary for review. A child-safety concern that cannot be submitted in-app
can be sent to [operated child-safety email address].

For immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Do not download,
forward or redistribute suspected CSAM in an attempt to report it.

## Response and enforcement

ChatHouse will assess child-safety reports, restrict access to known CSAM,
preserve relevant information where legally permitted and required, suspend or
terminate involved accounts, and report confirmed or suspected material and
conduct to the competent child-protection or law-enforcement authority where
required by applicable law.
