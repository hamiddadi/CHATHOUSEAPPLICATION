These Guidelines apply to profiles, live audio rooms, room titles, messages,
voice messages, reactions, clubs/houses and every other interaction on
ChatHouse. They form part of the ChatHouse Terms of Use.

## Respect and safety

Do not harass, threaten, bully, stalk, dox, discriminate against or impersonate
another person. Do not encourage violence, suicide, self-harm or dangerous
acts. Credible and imminent danger should be reported to local emergency
services as well as through ChatHouse's report tools.

## Sexual content and child safety

Do not sexually exploit or abuse a child, groom a child, sexualize a minor,
solicit sexual content from a minor, or create, possess, distribute or
facilitate child sexual abuse material. Attempts to evade detection or move
such activity to another service are also prohibited. See the separate
ChatHouse Child Safety Standards.

Non-consensual intimate content, sexual extortion and threats to share intimate
content are prohibited. [Confirm the operator's policy for lawful adult sexual
content and the Store age-rating position.]

## Hate, violence and illegal activity

Do not promote hatred or dehumanization based on protected characteristics.
Do not praise or facilitate terrorism, violent extremism, trafficking, illegal
weapons, controlled-substance sales, fraud or other illegal activity.

## Privacy, authenticity and intellectual property

Do not disclose another person's private information, record a room without all
required consent, operate a deceptive account, manipulate engagement, or share
content you do not have the right to use.

## Spam and platform abuse

Spam, phishing, malware, scams, unauthorized automation, scraping, security
evasion and attempts to circumvent blocks, bans or rate limits are prohibited.

## Reporting and blocking

- Use the in-app **Report** action on a user or room and choose the most accurate
  reason.
- Use **Block** to stop further interaction with a user.
- A room host or moderator can mute or remove disruptive participants where the
  feature permits it.
- For a safety issue that cannot be reported in-app, contact [operated safety
  email address]. Never send a password, OTP, token or full payment-card number.

The report tool is not an emergency service. Contact local emergency services
for an immediate threat.

## Review, enforcement and appeals

ChatHouse may restrict content or features, end a room, preserve relevant
evidence, warn a user, suspend or terminate an account, and notify competent
authorities when legally required. Severity, context, repeat behaviour,
immediate risk and applicable law influence the response.

Appeal a moderation decision through [verified in-app or support appeal route].
The operator must publish only a response-time commitment it can consistently
staff and evidence.
